<h1>Treasure Hunt</h1>

<p>Treasure hunt is a game in which you find antiques by digging using a shovel and sell it the antique appraiser. You will get money by selling your antiques and go to next level. (Max 4 levels).</p>

To play this game:

1. Use WASD to move
2. Look down and left click to dig
3. Go to the white person to sell antiques
   
Hint: Use "E" to sell the antiques when near the antique appraiser(white person)

Play the game at: https://vasarohan.itch.io/treasure-hunt
